# Wi-Fi Connection Failure

## Problem

A computer was unable to connect to a Wi-Fi network.

## Symptoms

- The computer could not establish a Wi-Fi connection.
- Internet access was unavailable.
- Other devices could be checked to determine whether the issue was isolated to the computer.

## Troubleshooting Process

### 1. Check the Wi-Fi connection

Confirmed that Wi-Fi was enabled on the computer and that the correct wireless network was selected.

### 2. Check the network connection

Checked whether the computer could detect available wireless networks.

### 3. Restart the connection

Disabled and re-enabled the Wi-Fi connection and attempted to reconnect to the network.

### 4. Restart the computer

Restarted the computer to clear temporary connection or system issues.

### 5. Check the router

Checked the router and confirmed whether other devices were able to connect to the network.

### 6. Test the connection

Attempted to reconnect to the Wi-Fi network and tested internet connectivity.

## Result

The troubleshooting process was used to identify whether the problem originated from the computer, its wireless configuration, or the network/router.
